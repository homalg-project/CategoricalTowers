  
  [1X14  [33X[0;0YQuotient  categories  of  algebroids  from  data tables using presheaves[101X
  [1Xcategories[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YLet  [23XA[123X  be  an  algebroid  over  a  commutative ring [23Xk[123X, given by finite data
  tables,   and   let   [23XI[123X  be  the  two-sided  ideal  generated  by  morphisms
  [23X\alpha_i:t_i\to  u_i[123X,  where  [23Xt_i[123X  and [23Xu_i[123X are objects of [23XA[123X. The aim of this
  construction  is  to  realize  the quotient algebroid [23XA/I[123X as a quotient of a
  presheaf  associated  with  [23XA[123X.  More  precisely, the ideal [23XI[123X gives rise to a
  subpresheaf  [23XF_I\subseteq  F_A[123X,  and the Hom-bifunctor of [23XA/I[123X is realized as
  the quotient presheaf [23XF_A/F_I[123X.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YMore precisely, regard the Hom-bifunctor of [23XA[123X as the presheaf[133X
  
  
  [24X[33X[0;6YF_A \in \mathrm{PSh}(A^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes_k A)[133X
  
  [124X
  
  [33X[0;0Ydetermined  by  its  values  on  the  objects  and  generating  morphisms of
  [23XA^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes_k A[123X: For two objects [23Xu[123X and [23Xt[123X of [23XA[123X, we have[133X
  
  
  [24X[33X[0;6YF_A(u^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes t)=\operatorname{Hom}_A(t,u).[133X
  
  [124X
  
  [33X[0;0YFor objects [23Xs,t,u,v[123X of [23XA[123X and morphisms [23Xf:s\to t[123X and [23Xg:u\to v[123X, the elementary
  tensor       [23Xg^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes       f:v^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes      s\to
  u^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes t[123X is mapped contravariantly to[133X
  
  
  [24X[33X[0;6Y\operatorname{Hom}_A(t,u)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Hom}_A(s,v),    \qquad
  h\longmapsto g\circ h\circ f.[133X
  
  [124X
  
  [33X[0;0YThe  left  and  right  actions commute by associativity, as displayed in the
  following  diagram.  Thus  [23XF_A[123X is [23XA[123X, through its Hom-modules and composition
  maps,    regarded    as    a    module   over   the   enveloping   algebroid
  [23XA^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes_k A[123X.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YFor  an  object  [23Xx[123X  of  [23XA^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes_k A[123X, let [23XP_x[123X denote its image
  under  the  Yoneda  embedding.  Such representable presheaves are projective
  objects. By the Yoneda lemma, every relation [23X\alpha_i[123X determines a morphism[133X
  
  
  [24X[33X[0;6Y\lambda_{\alpha_i}:P_{u_i^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes t_i}\longrightarrow F_A.[133X
  
  [124X
  
  [33X[0;0YThe  family  of  morphisms  [23X\lambda_{\alpha_i}[123X  induces  a morphism from the
  direct sum of their source objects,[133X
  
  
  [24X[33X[0;6Y\tau:\bigoplus_i P_{u_i^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes t_i}\longrightarrow F_A.[133X
  
  [124X
  
  [33X[0;0YDefine the ideal presheaf [23XF_I[123X and the quotient presheaf [23XF_{A/I}[123X by[133X
  
  
  [24X[33X[0;6YF_I:=\operatorname{im}(\tau),\qquad
  F_{A/I}:=F_A/F_I=\operatorname{coker}(\tau).[133X
  
  [124X
  
  [33X[0;0YFor  [23Xx=v^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes s[123X, the quotient sequence and its evaluation at
  [23Xx[123X   fit   into  the  following  commutative  diagram  with  exact  rows.  At
  [23Xv^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes  s[123X,  the  value  of  [23XF_I[123X  is  precisely the submodule
  [23XI(s,v)\subseteq\operatorname{Hom}_A(s,v)[123X spanned by all compatible two-sided
  composites of the [23X\alpha_i[123X. Hence[133X
  
  
  [24X[33X[0;6YF_{A/I}(v^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes      s)     =\operatorname{Hom}_A(s,v)/I(s,v)
  =\operatorname{Hom}_{A/I}(s,v).[133X
  
  [124X
  
  [33X[0;0YThus  [23XF_{A/I}[123X  is the Hom-bifunctor of the quotient algebroid, regarded as a
  presheaf on [23XA^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes_k A[123X via the quotient functor [23XA\to A/I[123X.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YThis  description  is  the mathematical basis of the method [10XQuotientCategory[110X
  for  algebroids  defined  by  data tables in the package [5XFpLinearCategories[105X.
  Indeed,  for  a  morphism [23Xm:s\to v[123X in [23XA[123X, set [23Xx:=v^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes s[123X and
  let  [23X\pi:F_A\to  F_{A/I}[123X be the cokernel projection. Membership in the ideal
  is characterized by[133X
  
  
  [24X[33X[0;6Ym\in I(s,v) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \lambda_m(x)\text{ factors through
  }\tau(x) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \pi(x)\circ\lambda_m(x)=0.[133X
  
  [124X
  
  [33X[0;0YWhen  these  equivalent conditions hold, the factorization is represented by
  the dashed lift in the following commutative diagram. Since composition in [23XA[123X
  is  encoded by finite matrices, this criterion is decided by linear algebra.
  Over  a  field,  the  quotient Hom-modules are computed as cokernels. Over a
  more  general computable ring, membership is decided by a lift through [23X\tau[123X,
  and   quotient  Hom-modules  are  represented  in  the  corresponding  Freyd
  category.  Thus  taking  a further quotient of an algebroid already given by
  data  tables  is  computed  directly  by linear algebra, without computing a
  noncommutative  Groebner  basis  or  applying  a  path-reduction  algorithm.
  Constructing  those  data  tables  from an earlier path presentation may, of
  course, have required a Groebner basis.[133X
